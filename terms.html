What ibet is

ibet lets two people stake SOL against each other on whether a Solana token's market cap will touch a target before a deadline. A Solana program holds both stakes in escrow and pays out automatically. ibet is not a counterparty to any bet and never holds funds outside that program.

How a bet is decided

  • Market cap means the token's price multiplied by its total supply.
  • A bet is won the moment the market cap touches the target — the high of a one-minute candle for a Higher bet, the low for a Lower one. It does not have to end there.
  • The creator is paid immediately on a touch, which can be long before the deadline. The taker wins only if the target is never touched in the whole window.
  • Settlement is automatic and final. There is no dispute, appeal or review, and neither player has any say in the outcome.
  • Prices come from a public market data provider. A second provider is used only to detect broken data, never to change an outcome.

Fees and limits

  • A platform fee is taken from the pot when a bet settles. The current rate is shown before you confirm any bet.
  • No fee is taken on a cancelled or refunded bet.
  • Stakes are capped per bet, and the program holds a limited total at any time. Both limits are published on chain and shown on the site.

If something goes wrong

If a bet is not settled within 48 hours of its deadline, anyone can trigger a refund and both sides get their own stake back with no fee deducted. An unmatched bet can be cancelled by its creator at any time. Neither of these can be blocked, including while the site is paused.

Your responsibility

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You are responsible for whether using ibet is lawful where you are. ibet is not available in some countries.
  • Any token can be bet on. Nobody screens them. Small tokens move violently and a single large trade can push a price through a target for one minute and fall straight back — that still counts.
  • Only stake what you can afford to lose.
  • You are responsible for your own wallet and keys. A transaction you approve cannot be reversed by anyone.

No warranty

ibet is provided as is, without warranty. Software, market data and blockchains can all fail. The per-bet and total limits exist to bound what any single failure can cost, and you should treat them as the maximum you might lose to one.
