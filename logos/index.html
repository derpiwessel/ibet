The loop

Every trade refills the vein.

No team bag gets the fees. They go straight back into the chart as buybacks, and the coins they buy go underground for holders to dig up.

01Trade

Every buy and sell pays a 1% fee. That is the only fuel this mine runs on.

02Buy back

The fee goes straight back into the chart and market-buys the coin. Nobody skims it.

03Bury

The bought-back coins don’t sit in a wallet. They get buried in the vein as ore.

04Mine

Holders mine the vein. Your share of every swing is your share of the supply. Claim whenever.
